**Tanjung Kaca** — an original open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city. Walk the streets and drive the grid, steal cars, outrun the police (roadblocks and a helicopter at high wanted levels), fight with fists and firearms, and play eight story missions from Rani the courier and Pak Darma the garage owner — plus repeatable delivery side jobs, a weapon shop, pickups, a minimap HUD, procedural WebAudio sound and music, and saves. Every name, character, street and story is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests: `game/` is plain static files.

Controls: WASD / arrows to move and drive, E to enter or steal a car, F to punch, mouse click to shoot, Q to switch weapons, P to pause, K to save, M to mute.
